This page explains how to delete your Stourify account and exactly what happens when you do. It is
the web route for making that request — you do not need the app installed to use it.

## The fastest way: delete it in the app

If you still have Stourify installed, you can delete your account yourself, immediately, without
asking anyone:

1. Open Stourify and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab, then **Settings**.
3. Scroll to **DANGER ZONE** and tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm with your **email address** and **password**.

The deletion happens straight away. Nothing is queued and nobody has to approve it.

## If you cannot use the app

If you have uninstalled Stourify, lost access to your device, or cannot sign in, email us and we
will delete the account for you:

**Send an email to [DELETION REQUEST EMAIL]**

- **Subject:** `Account deletion request`
- **Include:** the email address on the account, and your username (`@handle`) if you know it.

We will confirm that you control that email address before deleting anything — this check exists to
stop somebody else deleting your account. Once verified, we will complete the deletion and confirm
in writing within **[RESPONSE PERIOD, e.g. 30 days]**.

Do not send us your password. We will never ask for it by email.

## What is deleted, and when

### Straight away

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sign-in tokens | All revoked — every signed-in device is logged out |
| Your account | Disabled, and marked for erasure |
| Spots you added | Withdrawn from the service |
| Posts you published | Withdrawn from the service |
| Reviews you wrote | Withdrawn from the service |
| Your wishlist | Erased outright |
| Your explorer profile — username, bio, website, interests | Erased outright |
| Follows, in both directions | Erased outright — you follow nobody, nobody follows you |
| The app's local database on your device | Wiped |

### After 18 months

Everything still held — including the underlying account record, your name and your email address,
and any content retained alongside it — is **permanently erased**. This retention window exists so we
can deal with abuse reports, disputes and legal obligations that surface after an account has gone.

Routine backups are overwritten on their own schedule and may briefly outlast that date.

## Two things to know before you delete

**Your email address is reserved for 18 months.** Because the account record survives until the
retention window elapses, **you cannot register a new Stourify account with the same email address
until those 18 months have passed.** If there is a chance you will want to come back, consider simply
signing out rather than deleting.

**Deletion cannot be undone**, and it cannot be reversed from the app once confirmed.

## What is not deleted

- **Abuse reports you filed** stay in the moderation record, so that patterns of behaviour remain
  recognisable after a reporting account is gone.
- **Photographs already copied by other people** are outside our control. Media on Stourify is
  publicly reachable by design; deleting removes it from the service, but cannot retrieve copies
  someone else has already saved.
- **Records we are legally required to keep** are retained for as long as the law requires.

## Just want to pause?

The app also supports deactivating an account instead of deleting it. Deactivating signs you out and
hides the account without erasing anything, and it can be reversed by signing back in.
